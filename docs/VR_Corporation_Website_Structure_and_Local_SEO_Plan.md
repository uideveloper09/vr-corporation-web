Launch a fast, mobile-first static website that presents V R Corporation as the local cooling advisor — not merely an AC seller. ## 1. Search opportunity and planning assumptions

### 1.1 Current opportunity

A current search scan shows a fragmented result set: marketplace/directory pages, broad Sonipat repair pages, and a limited number of official dealer pages. An official Daikin page is visible for a Sonipat dealer, while Kharkhoda-specific results include general electronics listings and refrigeration/AC service pages. This creates an opportunity for V R Corporation to publish a stronger first-party local entity: a real showroom, real location, focused Daikin inventory, installation capability and ongoing service support.

> **Interpretation:** The opportunity is strongest at the Kharkhoda level. Sonipat terms are useful secondary targets, but they will be more competitive and should not dilute the homepage’s primary Kharkhoda focus.

### 1.2 Important assumptions

- The storefront is customer-facing, permanently signed and staffed during stated business hours.
- The exact legal/real-world business name used on signage is “V R Corporation”.
- Daikin authorization and brand-asset usage will be confirmed before publishing the site.
- Opening time is 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM; operating days still require confirmation.
- The final phone/WhatsApp number, email address and domain will be inserted before launch.
- The supplied map link is a coordinate pin, not an existing Google Business Profile.

### 1.3 Keyword-method note

The priorities in this plan are directional, based on business relevance, local intent, current search-result patterns and conversion potential. No search-volume figures are claimed. Before paid campaigns or large-scale content production, validate volumes and variants through Google Ads Keyword Planner, Search Console data after launch, and Google Business Profile performance data.

### 1.4 Positioning statement

V R Corporation helps homes, offices and commercial spaces in Kharkhoda and nearby Sonipat areas select, install and maintain the right Daikin cooling solution — with local showroom access and dependable support after switch-on.

### 1.5 Local search principles

Google states that local results are mainly influenced by relevance, distance and prominence. Complete and accurate profile information, verification, current hours, photos, reviews and owner responses can improve visibility. Distance cannot be optimized away, so the plan concentrates on stronger relevance and prominence within the genuine service footprint.

## 3. Local SEO keyword plan

Use natural language and one clear primary intent per page. “Near me” queries are driven heavily by location and Business Profile signals; do not force the phrase into every heading. The website should establish strong relevance while the verified profile supplies location, hours, categories, reviews and proximity context.

| Cluster | Target phrases | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Priority 1 — launch | daikin ac dealer in kharkhoda; daikin showroom in kharkhoda; ac dealer in kharkhoda; daikin ac near me; ac showroom near me | High local purchase intent |
| Priority 2 — residential | split ac dealer kharkhoda; inverter ac dealer kharkhoda; daikin split ac kharkhoda; best AC for room size kharkhoda | Product selection intent |
| Priority 2 — service | AC installation kharkhoda; AC repair kharkhoda; AC maintenance kharkhoda; AC AMC kharkhoda | Service and after-sales intent |
| Priority 2 — district | daikin ac dealer in sonipat; daikin showroom sonipat; ac dealer sonipat | Wider district discovery |
| Priority 3 — commercial | VRV VRF dealer sonipat; ductable AC supplier sonipat; commercial HVAC kharkhoda; chillers and AHU solutions sonipat | Higher-value B2B intent |
| Supporting questions | which AC tonnage for my room; inverter vs non-inverter AC; AC site survey; AC installation checklist; benefits of AC AMC | Informational trust and FAQ |

### 3.1 Keyword use rules

- Put the primary query naturally in the title tag, H1, opening copy and one relevant internal link — not in every paragraph.
- Use both spellings “Sonipat” and “Sonepat” only where natural; keep the official business address consistent.
- Use Kharkhoda as the homepage’s main locality and Sonipat as the district context.
- Do not claim “best”, “#1”, “top dealer” or guaranteed rankings without objective evidence.
- Do not create fake reviews, aggregate ratings or service-area addresses.

### 3.2 Content themes that differentiate the dealer

- Room and tonnage guidance before the sale
- Neat installation and site-readiness expectations
- Residential versus commercial cooling decisions
- AMC, preventive care and support after switch-on
- Real showroom access, location clarity and local service availability
- Daikin product categories explained without copying manufacturer text

### 7.3 LocalBusiness schema

Implement JSON-LD on the Home or Visit page using the most specific appropriate LocalBusiness subtype available, such as HVACBusiness, and include Store only when it accurately reflects the published business.

Recommended fields: `name`, `address`, `telephone`, `URL`, `image`, `logo`, `geo`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `areaServed`, `hasMap` and `sameAs`.

Validate through Google’s Rich Results Test and URL Inspection after launch.

> **Schema integrity:** Do not add aggregateRating, reviews, prices, opening days, social profiles or service claims that are not visibly supported on the page and factually verified.

## 8. Google Business Profile plan

Because customers can visit the signed showroom and the business also provides installation/service at customer locations, V R Corporation appears suited to a hybrid profile, subject to Google’s verification and current eligibility rules. Show the real storefront address and define only genuine service areas.

### 8.1 Profile creation sequence

1. Create or claim one profile for the real business location; search first to avoid duplicates.
2. Use the exact real-world name “V R Corporation”. Do not append “Daikin AC Dealer Kharkhoda” to the name unless that wording is consistently part of permanent signage and branding.
3. Enter the precise address and place the map pin on the storefront entrance.
4. Choose the narrowest accurate primary category available at setup time. Start by evaluating “Air conditioning store”; use secondary categories such as HVAC contractor or air-conditioning repair service only if they accurately describe active operations.
5. Add the final local phone, final website URL, operating days, 10:00 AM–7:00 PM hours and special hours.
6. Add up to 20 genuine service areas by city/postal area, not an invented radius.
7. Complete verification using the method offered by Google and retain ownership under the client’s business account.
8. Add products and services with clear descriptions, then upload authentic exterior, interior, product and team/process photos.

### 8.2 Suggested service list

- Split and inverter AC sales
- VRV/VRF systems
- Ductable AC solutions
- Chillers and AHU solutions
- AC installation
- AC repair and maintenance
- Annual maintenance contracts (AMC)
- HVAC consultancy and site assessment

### 8.3 Review and photo operating rhythm

- Ask real customers for an honest Google review after completed sales or service; never offer incentives or fabricate reviews.
- Reply to every review in a helpful, specific and privacy-safe way.
- Publish fresh, authentic photos regularly: storefront, product display, installations and seasonal readiness.
- Keep hours, services and temporary closures current. Correct profile edits promptly.

The goal is consistent, legitimate confirmation of the same entity — not mass submission to low-quality directories. Each citation should use the final approved name, address, phone, hours and URL.

### 9.1 Priority citation sources

- Official Daikin dealer/partner directory, if the dealership agreement permits and the listing is available
- Google Business Profile
- Apple Business Connect and Bing Places
- Relevant Indian business directories where a complete, owner-controlled listing can be maintained
- Local business associations, supplier/partner pages and legitimate Sonipat/Kharkhoda community sources
- Social profiles actually maintained by the business

### 9.2 Link and content opportunities

- Daikin partner/dealer listing linking to the canonical website
- Commercial project/vendor references where disclosure is permitted
- Local housing society or business-directory partnerships based on genuine service relationships
- Useful seasonal guides: pre-summer AC readiness, room-size planning and commercial cooling assessment

## 11. Measurement and acceptance criteria

### 11.1 Metrics to monitor

- Google Business Profile discovery queries, calls, website clicks and direction requests
- Search Console impressions, clicks, average position and indexed pages by query/page
- Organic enquiries by call, WhatsApp, form and showroom-direction click
- Review count, review recency, rating distribution and owner-response rate
- Conversion rate by landing page and device
- Core Web Vitals and mobile usability

### 11.2 Launch acceptance checklist

- [ ] Final domain, local phone/WhatsApp, email and operating days approved
- [ ] Name/address/phone/hours match across website and Business Profile
- [ ] Map pin opens at the storefront entrance
- [ ] Authorization wording and Daikin brand assets approved
- [ ] Every page has unique title, description and H1
- [ ] LocalBusiness schema validates with no fabricated properties
- [ ] Mobile layout, tap targets, forms, call and WhatsApp actions tested
- [ ] Sitemap submitted and priority pages requestable in Search Console
- [ ] Privacy notice and enquiry consent included
- [ ] No fake claims, reviews, prices, awards or ranking promises

### 11.3 Practical expectations

Do not promise a fixed Maps position or a guaranteed timeline. Local rankings vary by the searcher’s distance, query relevance, competitor prominence and Google’s systems. The first 90 days should establish a clean entity, accurate local signals and measurable enquiry pathways; ongoing reviews, photos, useful content and genuine local mentions support longer-term growth.
